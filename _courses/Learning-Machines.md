This course concerns designing robot systems that can sense their environment, act upon it, and improve their behavior to solve tasks.Previous years' assignment, to illustrate what the course involves: videoMultiple tasks/assignments should be delivered, and students have the freedom to choose different learning and optimization methods to work with.Students are expected to apply algorithms to optimize the controllers (brains) of the robots to solve the given tasks while utilizing the sensors as inputs.All tasks will be done in simulation and in hardware using Robobo robots: these are wheeled robots with fixed bodies that possess infrared sensors and a camera.For the simulations, a robot programming/simulation framework will be provided to students (Coppelia).It is possible in a given year/period that both the robot platform and its simulator may diverge from Robobos, with additional optimization of the robot’s morphology/body.Most of the course encompasses practical sessions with only a few lectures.
