(Upcoming course)
Learn to build intelligent, knowledge-aware robotic systems by bridging Knowledge Graphs and robotics. This course covers ontology modeling (OWL, RDF), SPARQL querying, and neuro-symbolic methods for knowledge acquisition. Through hands-on integration with ROS, you will apply advanced reasoning and planning techniques to solve real-world robotics challenges and culminate your experience by authoring an original scientific research paper.
