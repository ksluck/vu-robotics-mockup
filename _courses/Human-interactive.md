Human-Interactive Agent Learning (HIAL) is an area of interactive AI that focuses on developing agents (e.g., robots, artificial assistants) that can improve their task performance through interaction with humans. This course aims to cover basic HIAL principles and techniques, drawing from the fields of Machine Learning, Human Factors, Behavior Modelling, Robotics, and Design.
