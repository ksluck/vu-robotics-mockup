---
title: Adaptive Soft Grippers for Agriculture
funder: NWO (Dutch Research Council)
status: Active (2024 - 2028)
tags: Soft Robotics, Agriculture
---

This project develops novel soft robotic end-effectors capable of harvesting delicate crops, such as tomatoes and bell peppers, without causing bruising. By combining computer vision with compliant materials, the robots can adapt to varying crop shapes in real-time.
