Social Robotics focuses on designing embodied systems that can establish meaningful, long-term relationships with people in real-world settings. Working within the Social AI group at VU Amsterdam, this research specializes in child–robot interaction and healthcare, using participatory design and creative arts to co-develop companions for education, hospital support, and dementia care. By combining safe, agentic AI with rich dialogue and expressive interaction, the work creates robots that empower human connection and adapt meaningfully over time.
