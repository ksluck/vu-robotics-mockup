Swarm Robotics is an approach to robotics that draws inspiration from collective intelligence and complex systems, coordinating large groups of simple physical agents without centralized control. Ultimately, swarm robotics bridges theoretical complexity science and practical engineering—deepening our understanding of natural collective dynamics while guiding the design of scalable, fault-tolerant, and socially beneficial autonomous systems.
