Evolutionary Robotics (ER) and Artificial Life (ALife) are closely linked disciplines that use biological principles—such as natural selection, self-organization, and emergence—to synthesize lifelike behaviors in synthetic systems. Together, they bridge biology and engineering—using synthetic evolution to automate machine design while uncovering how intelligence, behavior, and physical form co-evolve in nature.
