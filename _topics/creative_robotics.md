Software Engineering for Creative Systems explores the design, execution, and long-term evolution of software within artistic, musical, and expressive domains. Bridging rigorous software architecture with human-computer interaction, this research investigates how computational tools and languages interface with the physical, tangible, and creative worlds, enabling novel mediums for creative expression while ensuring complex, code-based artworks and interactive systems remain robust, adaptable, and maintainable over time.
