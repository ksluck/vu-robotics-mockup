The growing complexity of robotics software makes it increasingly difficult to reason about cross-layer design decisions involving energy efficiency, performance, safety, and other quality attributes,
with direct consequences for whether the system can complete its mission at all. In particular, energy efficiency is a critical requirement for
battery-powered platforms.
