Cognitive Robotics intersects with Hybrid Intelligence by bringing symbolic approaches and knowledge engineering into the physical world. Rather than relying solely on black-box machine learning, this research investigates how knowledge graphs, semantic technologies, and neuro-symbolic reasoning can be integrated into robotic systems. By combining data-driven learning with structured, explicit human knowledge, this approach enables cognitive robots to not only adapt to complex environments but also explain their behaviors, reason abstractly, and collaborate transparently with humans.
