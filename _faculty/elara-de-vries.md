Prof. Dr. Elara de Vries leads the Cognitive Robotics lab at the Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. Her work focuses on bridging the gap between biological adaptability and mechanical design, ensuring robots can safely collaborate with humans in dynamic environments. 

Prior to joining the VU, she completed her postdoctoral research at MIT and has been the recipient of multiple ERC grants.
