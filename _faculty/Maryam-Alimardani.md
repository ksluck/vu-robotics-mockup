My research in Neuroadaptive Human-Robot Interaction and Brain-Computer Interfaces bridges neuroscience and robotics to explore how human brain signals can empower adaptive artificial systems. Based at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam and Hiroshi Ishiguro Laboratories, I investigate the use of EEG and cognitive state monitoring to create neuroadaptive technologies. By directly linking neural activity with machine behavior, I aim to enable robots and virtual agents to dynamically measure and respond to human engagement, attention, and mental states in real time-fostering more seamless, intuitive, and responsive human-machine collaboration.
