Mike Ligthart is an assistant professor in the Social AI research group at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam. His research develops social robots that interact meaningfully with people over longer periods of time, and he works most closely with children and the adults who support them. Together with children, hospitals, schools, and libraries, he has co-developed robots that support children through invasive medical procedures, offer personalised mathematics education, and hold richer conversations about books to stimulate reading motivation. A second line of his work concerns people living with dementia and their caregivers, asking how a robot can act as a catalyst for social contact between someone with dementia and the people around them.
