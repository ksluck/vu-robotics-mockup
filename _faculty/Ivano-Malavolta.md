My research focuses on software engineering, with a special emphasis on Green software and software architecture. I apply empirical methods to extract best software engineering practices for developing energy-efficient robotics software. I am the founder of the RoSE workshop series on Robotics Software Engineering (co-located with ICRA and ICSE) and area editor of robotics journals for software engineering submissions.
