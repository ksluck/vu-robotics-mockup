I am an Assistant Professor at Vrije Universiteit Amsterdam, where I lead a team of researchers dedicated to advancing the field of robot learning. My work explores a broad spectrum of topics within deep learning and general robot learning, including the development of world models for continuous control and novel approaches to policy synthesis. A particular area of passion for me is combining deep reinforcement learning with evolutionary principles for achieving generative robot design and behaviour learning. By developing AI-assisted design methodologies and advancing physical and embodied intelligence, our team investigates how to allow robots to dynamically co-adapt both their behavior and morphology.
