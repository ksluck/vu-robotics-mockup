My research is at the intersection of Evolutionary Robotics and Artificial Life, with the goal of advancing robot autonomous systems but also deepening our understanding of biological evolution. At the core of my work is exploring how environments shape the traits of evolving organisms, and how developmental processes can be embedded directly into the genetic representations of artificial life forms.
Some of the topics I’m interested on are embodied intelligence, environmental influences and environmental regulation, phenotypic plasticity, evo-devo, and gene expression.
