We are the ASE (Autonomous Systems Engineering) lab and we are teaching computer science students how to program and test smart systems. For this purpose we built miniature smart systems that mimic real life safety critical systems like 25+ selfdriving cars, 3 railways systems, one insulin pump, 15 smart homes, and a medical linear accelerator used for radiotherapy. Our VU-Rover won the NXP students race competition in 2024. We also built a smart home. Besides teaching, we also do research with the VU-Rover mainly on Bsc projects level on AI for indoors navigation, traffic congestion analysis, energy efficiency, etc. We did in the past research on distributed control algorithms like Blockchain used for railway management in collaboration with ProRail.
