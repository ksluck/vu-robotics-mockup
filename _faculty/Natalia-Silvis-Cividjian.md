I have been working at the VU since 2002. I like to design teaching material that guides students' first steps in building miniature smart systems like self-driving cars that recognize traffic signs and lights or social robots that recognize speech or even emotions. I am researching how to teach students not only how to program these robotic systems, but how to optimize them and test them to ensure that they are safe and will not harm people. I do this by applying a modern, systems-theoretic method for safety analysis. Our VU-rover reached perfection and won the 2024 car race student competition organized by NXP  in Hamburg. We work a lot in collaboration with the VU Beta electronics and mechanics groups and create beautiful robotic products.  Together we obtained in 2026 the Education Impact Award from the Faculty of Science. We are presenting our results at conferences on computer science and software engineering education.
