My research centers on collective intelligence, swarm robotics, decision-making in multi-agent systems, and mathematical modeling. I am fascinated by how large groups of relatively simple agents—whether robots or natural systems—can coordinate to solve complex tasks and exhibit emergent collective behaviors. By developing models and theoretical frameworks, I aim to uncover the principles that drive these processes, and in doing so, contribute to both the foundations of collective intelligence and its real-world applications.
